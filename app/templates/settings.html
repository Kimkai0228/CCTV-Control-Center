How compression is chosen

QuickSync is auto-detected at runtime: QSV/MFX first, then VAAPI. If the GPU probe fails, jobs fall back to libx264 immediately.

Lower quality values produce better video but larger files. Use Re-probe after changing hardware or drivers.
